**Leonida Legend** — an offline trophy guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Every trophy is shown with its official name, description and grade, plus a guide written for this entry in its own words that names its sources (the official PlayStation description, and the trophy list with its tracked counts as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles).

**Round 2** rebuilds the guide on sources fetched and read in full: the PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap, the Exophase PS5 list, GAMES.GG's complete list and Press Start's full list, all named in an in-app sources registry (S1–S4). Each trophy now carries 3–6 numbered steps, and **all 233 steps end with a source tag**; 55/55 guides are backed by 2+ sources. The 28 PSN-hidden trophies are flagged from GAMES.GG's 27 shown / 28 hidden split. Trophies with a count get an interactive **YOUR COUNT** tracker (saved in the browser), an audit bar at the top computes the citation totals live from the data, and research corrected round 1 where it was wrong: only Positive Mental Attitude is potentially missable (the Chapter 1 balcony meditation), and the Andy/Billy/Tyrone questlines do **not** count toward Strangers & Freaks. A five-stage roadmap leads to the Platinum: story chapters and named missions first, the big counts (250 stunts, 42 shooting-range golds, 100 WAiNK locations, 52 areas…) swept district by district, partner and one-off moment trophies banked before the finale, then the final mission and 100% completion. Sixteen trophies carry a ⚠ CAUTION flag built from their official descriptions — tied to one named mission, one named person, a unique animal, a choice or a single moment — so you do them when they first appear. The tracker saves progress in your browser (localStorage), filters by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online — the official list has no online trophies) and by grade, searches, hides story trophies behind a spoiler shield until they are earned, and exports/imports progress as a file. Original look only: no Rockstar logo and no official artwork, and the app makes no network requests.

**Round 3** goes one level deeper, from trophy-level steps to item-level checklists, built from eight more PowerPyx pages fetched and read in full (registry S5–S12): all 10 meditation spots, the 12 shooting-range courses, the 24 stunt jumps published so far, all 18 card sets, all 14 drone-race locations and the 7 landmarks published so far. Six counted trophies now carry a per-item checklist — **85 items in total** — and ticking an item feeds that trophy's YOUR COUNT by the item's real weight (a meditation spot counts 1–2 meditations, a range course counts its 3–4 golds). The step count rises to **239, still 100% cited**, and the registry grows to 12 sources. Round 3 also adds the complete **35-line 100% completion checklist** for A Long Road and a **Sources Differ** panel with 8 entries where the fetched pages set two different bars — trophy vs 100% (250 vs 300 stunts, 3 vs 15 card sets, 20 vs 30 race wins, 557 vehicles in the game vs the 277 base models that count), the still-unconfirmed status of the Chapter 1 meditation, and one flat wording clash it corrects: the "12 Shooting Range Locations" are 12 challenges at 2 ranges. Where a source list is still a work in progress (24 of 130 stunt jumps, 7 of 31 landmarks), the guide labels its checklist partial instead of presenting it as complete.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts          # serves app/ at http://localhost:8080
```

or open `app/index.html` in a desktop browser with any static server rooted at `app/`. Hand-in: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts` copies `app/` and `screenshots/` to the output and writes `dossier.json` with the vendored `vendor/makefun-frontend.ts`, unchanged from the kit. `shots.json` describes the four screenshots in `screenshots/`.
