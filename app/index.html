Roadmap to the Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

Five stages, built from the official descriptions and the fetched roadmap in the registry: PowerPyx rates this platinum 4/10, roughly 200 hours, one playthrough — no mission replay, and cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep a save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots) [S1][S3]. The one potentially missable trophy is Positive Mental Attitude — the Chapter 1 balcony meditation at Jason's house [S1][S3]. The wider ⚠ CAUTION list below is this tracker's safety net: do flagged trophies when they first appear.

  1. Stage 1 · Arrival. Gone Fishing, Chapter 1, Breakfast Run. ⚠ Accept Lucia's balcony meditation at Jason's house the morning after Down to Earth — permanently gone after Chapter 1 [S1].
  2. Stage 2 · Rising. Chapters 2–4 and the finale missions. Strands as you meet them: Kenton (Key Lento), Billy (Mudclub), Andy (Lucia, Belville), Randy's 16-car Wish List, Tyrone's labs (Jason), Bret's hunts (Jason).
  3. Stage 3 · Open Leonida. The counts, swept district by district: 250 of 397 stunts · 42 range golds at 12 ranges · 14 drone-race locations · 20 races · 18 meditation spots · 3 of 18 card sets · 100 WAiNK scans · 100 vehicles scanned · 52 areas unfogged · 50 of 80 challenges · 8 garages ($160,000) · $250,000 laundered.
  4. Stage 4 · Partner, crime & moments — before the finale. Partner trophies from Coffee Break on, 5 of 8 bank vaults, the VCIA plane at E3/E4, the Mile Zero tanks, the Snap Gun jewellery heist, 1 operation per gang (9), Combat Focus ×5.
  5. Stage 5 · Finale & 100%. The final mission (Gold), then the checklist under Progress > Stats: 200/277 vehicle scans, 100/255 animals studied, any 70/80 challenges, 100 optional missions — and the Platinum unlocks itself.
