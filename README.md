**Leonida Legend** — a fully offline trophy guide and progress tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Each trophy is presented with its official name, description and grade, alongside a guide written for this entry in its own words and the sources that guide rests on (the official PlayStation description, and the trophy list with its tracked counts as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles).

**Round 2** rebuilt the guide on sources that were fetched and read in full: the PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap, the Exophase PS5 trophy list, GAMES.GG's complete list and Press Start's full list — all four named in an in-app sources registry (S1–S4). Every trophy gained 3–6 numbered steps, and **all 233 steps ended with a source tag**; 55/55 guides are backed by 2 or more sources. The 28 PSN-hidden trophies are flagged from GAMES.GG's 27 shown / 28 hidden split. Counted trophies received an interactive **YOUR COUNT** tracker (kept in the browser), an audit bar across the top computes the citation totals live from the data, and the research corrected round 1 wherever it had been wrong: only Positive Mental Attitude is potentially missable (the Chapter 1 balcony meditation), and the Andy/Billy/Tyrone questlines do **not** count toward Strangers & Freaks. A five-stage roadmap leads to the Platinum — story chapters and named missions first, the big counts (250 stunts, 42 shooting-range golds, 100 WAiNK locations, 52 areas…) swept district by district, partner and one-off moment trophies banked before the finale, then the final mission and 100% completion. Sixteen trophies carry a ⚠ CAUTION flag derived from their official descriptions — tied to one named mission, one named person, a unique animal, a choice or a single moment — so you handle them when they first appear. The tracker stores progress in your browser (localStorage), filters by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online — the official list contains no online trophies) and by grade, searches, hides story trophies behind a spoiler shield until they are earned, and exports/imports progress as a file. Original look only: no Rockstar logo, no official artwork, and the app makes no network requests.

**Round 3** went one level deeper, from trophy-level steps down to item-level checklists, built from eight further PowerPyx pages fetched and read in full (registry S5–S12): all 10 meditation spots, the 12 shooting-range courses, the 24 stunt jumps published so far, all 18 card sets, all 14 drone-race locations and the 7 landmarks published so far. Six counted trophies now carry a per-item checklist — **85 items in total** — and ticking an item feeds that trophy's YOUR COUNT by the item's real weight (a meditation spot is worth 1–2 meditations, a range course its 3–4 golds). The step count rose to **239, still 100% cited**, and the registry grew to 12 sources. Round 3 also added the complete **35-line 100% completion checklist** for A Long Road and a **Sources Differ** panel with 8 entries where the fetched pages set two different bars — trophy vs 100% (250 vs 300 stunts, 3 vs 15 card sets, 20 vs 30 race wins, 557 vehicles in the game vs the 277 base models that count), the still-unconfirmed status of the Chapter 1 meditation, and one flat wording clash it corrects: the "12 Shooting Range Locations" are 12 challenges at 2 ranges. Where a source list is still a work in progress (24 of 130 stunt jumps, 7 of 31 landmarks), the guide labels its own checklist partial instead of presenting it as complete.

## How this guide was built

The research came first. Each source in the registry — the PowerPyx roadmap and its sub-pages, the Exophase list, the GAMES.GG list, the Press Start list — was fetched and read in full, not skimmed from search snippets, and only facts that page actually states were allowed into the data. Every guide was then written fresh for this entry in its own words: sources are cited, never copied. Each of the 239 steps ends with the registry keys of the sources it rests on, so any claim in the app can be traced back to the page it came from in one hop. The numbers the app displays about itself — step totals, citation coverage, item counts, hidden and caution flags — are not typed into the page by hand; the audit bar computes them from `app/data/trophies.json` at load time, so the audit cannot drift away from the data it describes. Where two fetched sources disagreed, the disagreement itself was documented in the Sources Differ panel rather than silently resolved, and where a source admitted its own list was incomplete or a method "currently unknown", the guide repeats that admission instead of inventing a method.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts          # serves app/ at http://localhost:8080
```

or open `app/index.html` in a desktop browser with any static server rooted at `app/`. Hand-in: `mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts` copies `app/` and `screenshots/` to the output and writes `dossier.json` with the vendored `vendor/makefun-frontend.ts`, unchanged from the kit. `shots.json` describes the four screenshots in `screenshots/`.
