Roadmap to the Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

Five stages, built from the official descriptions. Story first, counts in parallel, partner & one-off moments banked before the finale, then 100%. Missable method: a trophy tied in its official description to one named mission, one named person, a unique animal, a choice or a single moment is flagged ⚠ CAUTION — do it when it first appears and keep a save before it.

  1. Stage 1 · Arrival. Gone Fishing, Chapter 1, Breakfast Run. Bank the Ocean Beach cocktail (In The Beginning…) while it is in front of you.
  2. Stage 2 · Rising. Chapters 2–4, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory. Finish stranger strands as you meet them: Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, and Bret's unique-animal hunt.
  3. Stage 3 · Open Leonida. The counts, swept district by district: 250 stunts · 42 shooting-range golds · 14 drone-race wins · 20 races · 18 meditation spots · 3 card sets · 100 WAiNK locations · 100 vehicles · 52 areas · 50 challenges · 8 garages · $100,000 spent · $250,000 laundered.

  5. Stage 5 · Finale & 100%. The final mission (Gold), then close the last counts for A Long Road — 100% Completion (Gold) — and the Platinum unlocks itself.
